# Settings artwork

The app and symbolic SVGs use the original geometry from the approved Aerial
companion mock. Runtime symbols are Qt Quick Shapes (window) or QPainter (tray),
not SVG loads. PNG app sizes are embedded as the window icon; both SVGs are
installed under `hicolor/scalable` (`apps/org.aeriallock.AerialSettings.svg`,
`status/org.aeriallock.AerialSettings-symbolic.svg`) for launchers and desktops.

Theme previews are solid-background renders of the bundled themes, copied from
the approved mock. They contain no Apple/community footage or private data.
Theme and font notices remain in Themes/ATTRIBUTION.md and Themes/fonts.
The mock's comparison fonts and Noctalia reference crop are not shipped.
